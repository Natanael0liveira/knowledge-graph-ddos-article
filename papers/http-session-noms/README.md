# NOMS submission

*Calibrated Scoping of Application-Layer DDoS Mitigation with a Session-Centric
Knowledge Graph* (title of the round-8 version; a reframing is in progress).

## Layout

```
pp. 1–8    main text (Sections I–VII)   <- AT the 8-page limit
p. 9       acknowledgment, references (37 entries), appendices from p. 10
pp. 10–12  appendices A–F
total      12 pages                     <- AT the 12-page limit
```

Appendices: **A** runtime construction and `relatedBy_*` instantiation ·
**B** weight calibration · **C** additional limitations, laboratory captures,
KLAGE, the learned representation across stack counts · **D** cost model, window
sensitivity and CDN-scale partitioning · **E** the rule per window and the
production details (WAF agreement, other baselines, the beta-binomial background,
known fleets) · **F** reproducibility.

> **Both limits are reached.** The body ends at the bottom of page 8 and page 12 is
> full, so any new sentence needs a cut. The body is within the limit only if the
> Acknowledgment sits on page 8 or at the top of page 9's left column.

Section structure follows the pattern observed in NOMS papers: Introduction ·
Related Work · Session-Centric Knowledge Graph · Evaluation Methodology ·
Results · Discussion and Limitations · Conclusion.

## Build

```bash
pdflatex article && bibtex article && pdflatex article && pdflatex article
```

Three files are vendored in this directory so that local and Overleaf builds are
identical:

- `IEEEtran.cls` and `IEEEtran.bst`, because the local TeX Live is the *basic*
  scheme and does not ship them.
- `stfloats.sty` (from `sttools`, LPPL), which allows double-column floats at the
  bottom of a page. Without it Table I and the two full-width figures can only
  sit at page tops, and they stack.

The preamble also falls back on `lmtt` when URW Courier (`pcr`) metrics are
absent, which avoids Type 3 bitmap fonts in the PDF. On Overleaf and full
installations neither fallback fires.
